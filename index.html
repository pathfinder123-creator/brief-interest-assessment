The Six Interest Types

RIASEC, in brief

RIASEC is a way of describing the kinds of activities and work settings people tend to find interesting. Your strongest three areas form your Interest Code.

Realistic — hands-on, mechanical, outdoors
Investigative — analytical, curious, research-driven
Artistic — creative, expressive, original
Social — helping, teaching, caring for others
Enterprising — leading, persuading, business-minded
Conventional — organized, detail-oriented, structured
Assessment

Rate each activity according to how interesting it is to you, not whether you can perform the activity or want to make a career of it.
